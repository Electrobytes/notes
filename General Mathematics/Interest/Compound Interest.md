Compound Interest also considers the accumulation of interest over time.

Formula:
$$
A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{(r)(t)}
$$
Where $A$ is the resulting maturity value, $r$ is the interest rate in decimal form. The $n$ refers to how frequent the interest will be added on top of the maturity value.

| frequency     | $n$ |
| ------------- | --- |
| annually      | 1   |
| semi-annually | 2   |
| quarterly     | 4   |
| monthly       | 12  |
| weekly        | 52  |
| daily         | 365 |
