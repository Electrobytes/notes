Resistance is how much an electric circuit or object resists the flow of electric charges and can be measured with ohms ($\Omega$).
$$
R = \rho \frac{L}{A}
$$
The formula above shows the relationship between the properties of the wire and the resulting resistance. $\rho$ refers to the resistivity of the material of the wire measured in $\Omega/\text{m}$, $A$ is the cross-sectional area of the wire measured in $\text{m}^2$, while $L$ describes the length of the wire.
