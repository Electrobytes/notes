Waves are measurable disturbances found in nature. It is typically classified into two: [[Electromagnetic Wave|electromagnetic waves]] and [[Mechanical Wave|mechanical waves]], and these can be either [[Transverse Wave|transverse]] or [[Longitudinal Wave|longitudinal]]. Its properties are governed by the equation below.
$$
v=\lambda f
$$
Where $v$ is the speed of light in a medium, $v=c$ if it is located inside a vacuum; $\lambda$ is the wavelength, or the distance between two peaks, throughs, equilibrium, or any other reference points; and $f$ is the frequency of the wave, or the number of cycles in a given period of time.
