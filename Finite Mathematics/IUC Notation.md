The IUC notation goes as follows: **p** or **c** for primitive and centered respectively,  and the number after it is the order of rotation. ($\frac{360\degree}{n}$) The succeeding characters are either **m** or mirror, **g** for glide reflection, or **1** as a placeholder for no reflection done for a particular kind. The reflections denoted in the notation and by order are horizontal and vertical reflection.
